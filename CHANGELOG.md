### 2026-11-15 — Phone Motion (Gyroscope 360° Look), Coral Hind (*Cephalopholis miniata*), Proportional Fauna & Ethology
- **Mobile Phone Motion (Gyroscope / `DeviceOrientation`) 360° Look**: Added a gimbal-lock-free quaternion phone-motion look controller (`enableGyro` / `toggleGyro` / `recenterGyro`) with a compact mobile floating pill (`📱 Motion` / `⌖` Recenter) and HUD camera control (`Phone Motion: Off / On (360°)`), iOS 13+ `DeviceOrientationEvent.requestPermission()` support, portrait & landscape screen-orientation recalibration, touch-drag offset blending, and Free-Ride Tour look-around without interrupting the automated tour.
- **Coral Hind Grouper Upgrade (*Cephalopholis miniata*, `דקרן האלמוגים`)**: Replaced the previous grouper with an anatomically sculpted vermilion Coral Hind matching Red Sea / FishBase specimens, featuring faint oblique russet saddle bars, dense pinpoint dark-rimmed sky-blue ocelli over high-resolution subdivided body & median fins (`132×84`), golden-orange unspotted distal pectorals, and electric-blue trailing margins.
- **FishBase-Proportional Fauna Size Alignment**: Calibrated scale across all 16 swimming species and rays/turtles from `7.7 cm` Blue-Green Chromis (`chromis`), `7.9 cm` Cleaner Wrasse (`cleanerwrasse`), `8.5 cm` Lyretail Anthias (`pseudanthias`), and `9.2 cm` Two-Band Anemonefish (`amphiprion`) through `12.7–13.0 cm` Crown & Exquisite Butterflyfishes (`chaetodon`, `chaetodonaustriacus`), `16.1 cm` Sergeant Major (`abudefduf`), `19.7 cm` Sand Lizardfish (`lizardfish`), `20.9 cm` Suez Fusilier (`caesio`), `23.9 cm` Goatfish (`goatfish`), `30.3 cm` Lionfish (`pterois`), and `38.0 cm` Coral Hind (`grouper`) to pelagic Trevally (`59.3 cm`), Bluespotted Ribbontail Ray, Green Sea Turtle, and Spotted Eagle Ray.
- **Smaller, Slow-Creeping Diurnal *Diadema* Sea Urchins**: Reduced *Diadema setosum* test diameter to `~11.5 cm` (`~50 cm` spine span) and added authentic evening/night bommy-crevice sheltering vs. peak-day algal floor grazing with glacial `<= 2.6 cm/s` benthic tube-foot locomotion.
- **3D Rock & Spatial-Hash Inter-Animal Collision Avoidance**: Built a baked 3D voxel + horizontal EDT signed-distance field (`rockCollider`) and zero-allocation 3D spatial hash (`animalCollider`) so swimmers, benthic invertebrates, and cleaning wrasses never penetrate rocks, clip sand, bury, or pass through one another.
- **Long-Range Cross-Reef Butterflyfish Patrols & Calm Upstream Planktivores**: Paired both Crown and Exquisite Butterflyfishes (`chaetodon` & `chaetodonaustriacus`, `1` bonded pair each by default) on 10-stop cross-reef coral circuits, and stabilized calm `pseudanthias`, `chromis`, and `abudefduf` facing upstream into local reef flow.
- **Overhead Water-Column Depth Optics & Selective UI View Modes**: Increasing `diveDepth` adds overhead water mass (`uSurfaceY = 2.8 + diveDepth`) above the fixed seafloor while attenuating direct beam sunlight, caustic contrast, and cast shadows through real slant optical path length; expanded `H` Cinema Mode into a frosted pill (`1–5` / `V` / `H`) supporting `All`, `Dashboard`, `Controls`, `360° Fine`, `ColorChecker`, and `Hide`.
